name: Discovery source constraints
description: Legal and availability constraints encountered when expanding Saudi career discovery
---

Do not use Bing's public search RSS feed as a production discovery API: its returned copyright notice restricts reuse to display within a personal, non-commercial RSS aggregator. The official PIF site and sitemap returned Cloudflare 403 from this environment during the September 2026 investigation; do not bypass the block or infer an active opening from portfolio membership.

**Why:** Broad search and current PIF membership require authorized, accessible sources; snippets and unverified seed lists do not establish current employer openings.

**How to apply:** Recheck official access and terms if circumstances change. Until an authorized runtime search service and an accessible official PIF portfolio feed are available, disclose incomplete coverage and count unsearched PIF employers as zero.
