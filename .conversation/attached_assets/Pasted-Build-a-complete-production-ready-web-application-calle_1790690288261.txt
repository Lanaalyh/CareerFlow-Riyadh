Build a complete, production-ready web application called CareerFlow Riyadh.

The application is a smart career and internship assistant designed specifically for university students looking for opportunities ONLY in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

The core idea is:

Upload my CV once → enter my information once → find all relevant open opportunities in Riyadh → personalize my CV/cover letter/application → let me review → help me apply.

The application should search beyond LinkedIn and should collect opportunities from multiple legitimate sources, including official company career pages, permitted job platforms, training platforms, and Saudi-focused opportunity platforms.

One important source to support is Ataba (عتبة), which publishes training and career opportunities. Treat Ataba as one of the opportunity sources, not the only source.

Do not depend on LinkedIn.
